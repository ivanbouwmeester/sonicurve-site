Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Sonicurve Uploader is a desktop tool used by its operator to upload original Sonicurve videos to social platforms.

Information and permissions

When TikTok authorization is used, the application may receive basic profile information and authorization tokens required to upload video drafts. These permissions are used only to provide the requested upload functionality.

Data sharing

Sonicurve Uploader does not sell personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to provide TikTok authentication and content-upload functionality.

Data retention

Authentication credentials may be stored locally on the operator's device so the application can remain connected. They can be removed by disconnecting the application and deleting locally stored credentials.
